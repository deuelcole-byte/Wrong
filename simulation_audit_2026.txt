FIX APPLIED
-----------
Replaced direct opponent-counting approach with CDF-based method:
  1. Generate 500 opponent brackets per tournament sim
  2. Score all opponents, build empirical score CDF
  3. P(win | pool_size=N) = CDF(our_score)^(N-1)

This correctly scales win probability with pool size without
needing to generate N-1 brackets for each N.

N=10000 CAVEAT
--------------
  With 500 opponent samples, CDF resolution = 1/500 = 0.2%.
  P(win | beat 499/500) at N=10000 = 0.998^9999 ≈ 0 (essentially zero).
  P(win | beat 500/500) at N=10000 = 1.0^9999 = 1.0.
  So the 0.80% win rate at N=10000 represents the fraction of sims
  where our bracket beat ALL 500 sampled opponents — an upper bound,
  not the true P(rank 1 in 10000). For accurate N=10000, would need
  ~50,000+ opponent samples per sim. Results for N=50, 200, 1000
  are reliable. N=10000 should be treated as approximate.

FLAT CURVE SURVIVAL
-------------------
  The flat curve finding SURVIVES the fix in absolute terms:
  chalk vs optimal differs by only 0.11-0.20pp across all pool sizes.
  However, in RELATIVE terms, the curve steepens with pool size:
    N=50:    +2.2% improvement over chalk (flat)
    N=200:   +6.8% improvement (moderate)
    N=1000:  +28.5% improvement (material)

  Conclusion: At larger pools, the upset budget matters more in relative
