KEY DATA GAPS
==============
- Beyer Speed Figures: Not available from any free source. Beyer figures are published
  exclusively by Daily Racing Form (subscription required). The "113" figure for Crazy
  Cavalier from Horse Racing Nation is their proprietary speed figure, NOT a Beyer.
- Running positions at each call (numeric): Requires Equibase charts or DRF PPs, both
  gated. Only qualitative trip descriptions recovered from race narratives.
- Beaten lengths at each call: Same — requires Equibase charts.
- Trip/trouble notes: Only available as narrative descriptions from race recap articles.
  Structured trip note data (e.g., "steadied", "checked", "wide") is typically only in
  Equibase charts (gated).
- Historical race data (2020-2021): Considerably harder to find in free sources than
  recent (2023-2026) data. Golden Gate Fields (closed 2024) and older Woodbine cards
  are poorly indexed outside of Equibase.
